Welcome to Ellijay, Georgia

Where the sweet tea is strong, the gossip travels fast, and love is always just around the corner.

In the Notes Between Us series, co-written with DeAnna Kaye Fields, you'll discover a close-knit community where music brings people together, faith carries them through life's challenges, and second chances arrive when they're least expected.

Seven interconnected stories. One unforgettable mountain town.

Meet the First Story
Coming February 1, 2027

Notes of Home

Sometimes the road to love leads you right back where you started.

When Ellie Calhoun returns to Ellijay after her Nashville dreams unravel, she finds familiar faces, unexpected hope, and Jay—a disciplined music director who challenges her at every turn.

Between choir rehearsals, community, and a few sparks neither of them can ignore, Ellie discovers that home might be more than a place.
