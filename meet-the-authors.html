Meet JB & DeAnna

JB Juke and DeAnna Kaye Fields join their creative voices to tell stories filled with memorable characters, heartfelt connections, and unexpected adventures.

Together, they co-write the small-town romance series Notes Between Us and the upcoming Wonderland-inspired romantic fantasy trilogy Wonder of Nonsense.

The JB+D logo identifies their co-authored projects. JB Juke also writes individual works, including Wen Rising and The Cardinal Patrons.
