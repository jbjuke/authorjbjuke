The Prompt: A Short Story Anthology

A standalone collection of suspenseful short stories by nine independent authors.

Standalone · Short Story Anthology

The clock is ticking. The place is waiting.

What happens when a diverse group of indie authors takes on the same writing prompt? Each story begins with a character returning to an old, abandoned location they swore never to visit again. They must retrieve a specific object before a strict midnight deadline.

Explore abandoned halls, buried memories, impossible choices, and secrets that only surface after dark. Every journey is different, but every one begins with a return that should never have happened.

Co-authors: T.I. Queen, Lynn Sullivan, Nicole Sisson, Daniel J Wright, Jennifer Hardman, J.R. Echo, G.M. Luttrell, JB Juke, and Brynne Aisling-Rowan

This anthology was created to support a charitable cause benefiting terminally ill animals and three cat colonies.

Availability: Amazon only at this time.
