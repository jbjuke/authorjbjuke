Coming Soon · YA Romantic Fantasy

Wonderland is about to get wonderfully complicated.

Long after Alice’s adventures, Marigold unexpectedly lands in Wonderland, turning Tabbott Hannover’s carefully unsettled life upside down. The Mad Hatter’s great-nephew has plans of his own—but Wonderland rarely respects anyone’s plans.

As Marigold and Tabbott navigate nonsense, danger, and an unexpected connection, their story stretches across worlds in a whimsical new romance trilogy.

Written by: JB Juke and DeAnna Kaye Fields
